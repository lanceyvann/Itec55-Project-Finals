PitStop OS — Concept & Quick Guide

Story Mode vs. OS Mode Terminology

Both views run the exact same live operating system simulation. Use the View: Story Mode / OS Mode button in the header to switch labels anytime.

Story Mode (Tire Workshop) OS Mode (Operating System) What It Represents
Shop Operations Manager OS Kernel Manages jobs, queues, syscalls, and hardware locks
Active Service Bay CPU Executes 1 active process/job per simulation tick
Vehicle Service Job Process An independent work order with its own ID & burst
Vehicle Service Record PCB (Process Control Block) Stores ID, parent, state, priority, registers & metrics
Mechanic Service Task Thread (TCB) Sub-tasks sharing the job's memory/work order
Equipment Lock Mutex Lock Exclusive lock on Tire Changer or Wheel Balancer
Service Bay Changeover Context Switch (CS) Switching the CPU/bay from one job to another
Checked In / Waiting for Service / Undergoing Service / Waiting for Equipment / Completed New / Ready / Running / Waiting / Terminated 5-State Process Lifecycle

Quick Controls & Standout Features

  • Playback & Scheduling: Use Start, Pause, or Step (+1 Tick) and compare FCFS, SJF, Round Robin (with configurable Quantum), and Priority across 3 shop presets.
  • Process CRUD & Hierarchy: Click + Add Vehicle Job / + Add Process to create jobs, Edit to modify bursts/priority/name, + Child to spawn a linked PPID sub-job, Terminate to halt execution, or Delete to remove a record.
  • "Why?" Explainer & Time Machine: Click any Gantt block to see why that job was chosen at that tick, jump to its snapshot, scrub past ticks, or click Resume Here to branch.
  • Deadlock Demo & Syscalls: Trigger a 2-job circular wait on the Tire Changer & Wheel Balancer and resolve it, or test manual createThread(), joinThread(), requestIO(), and yieldProcess() traps.
